2 · WHO CARRIES THE KEY — THREE FORM FACTORS
The person's tag can be purpose-built, bolted onto what they already wear, or just software. The trade is always the same one: LoRa range and hardening vs. cost and adoption.

Radio & rangeOnboard LoRa SX1262 — km-scale to the interrogatorOnboard LoRa — keeps the range, pairs to phone over BLE for UINo LoRa — cellular / Wi-Fi / BLE only (short) unless a LoRa dongle is added
Location (GNSS)Dedicated u-blox MAX-M10SBorrows the paired phone/watch GPS; coarse RF otherwiseDevice GPS (good) + maps built in

RuggednessIP68, heat / shock ratedModerate, pottableConsumer-grade
AdoptionAnother device to issue & chargeRides existing kit, minimalInstant, zero extra hardware, scales free
Best forCombat, wildfire — range & hardening matterUSAR, fast issue, low cost, bulk unit roll-outRear areas, training, command staff where there's coverage
Est. cost / unit~$150–320~$60–120~$0 extra HW (dev only) · +$40–80 LoRa dongle for range
Takeaway: LoRa is the differentiator — a phone or watch alone can't reach the drone at range, so add a small LoRa dongle and option C converges on option B. Mix per role: hardened bands at the edge, clip-ons for bulk issue, apps for rear / coverage areas. All three run the identical 32-byte HMAC exchange.
